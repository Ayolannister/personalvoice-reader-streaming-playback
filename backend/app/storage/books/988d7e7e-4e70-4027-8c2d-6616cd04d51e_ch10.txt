About the Author
David Kim is the discipleship and formation pastor at WestGate Church in
Silicon Valley, California. He is the author of a children’s book about
change, selected for Oprah’s Favorite Things List in 2020. He completed
his master of divinity and master of theology from Gordon-Conwell
Theological Seminary and cares deeply about the intersection of spiritual
formation, theology, and the cultivation of authentic Christian communities.
He lives with his wife and two daughters in San Jose, California.
